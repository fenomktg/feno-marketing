    Frequently asked

    How long does the Smartbrush battery last?

    Up to 21 days on a single charge with normal twice-daily use. The wireless charger in the box tops it up overnight.

    Can I cancel my Subscribe & Save plan anytime?

    Yes, after your Smartbrush is paid off (12 months of monthly billing, or immediately on the annual plan). If you cancel earlier, the remaining device balance is due at cancellation. After payoff, refills and Feno Premium continue until you cancel — no lock-in.

    How does the dentist sync work?

    In the Feno app, you generate a share link or invite your dentist by email. They see your biomarker history, scan trends, and brushing patterns in a clinician-friendly view. Available with Feno Premium (included in Subscribe & Save, $10/month add-on on One Time).

    What if the TrueFit Mouthpiece doesn't fit right?

    The first one is molded from an in-app scan after your bundle arrives. If the fit isn't right, we'll re-mold and ship a replacement free of charge. Covered under the 30-day money-back guarantee.

    Is the app compatible with my phone?

    iOS 16 or later, Android 12 or later. The scanner uses Bluetooth Low Energy — no special accessories needed.

    How accurate are the biomarker readings?

    The Smartbrush scanner is calibrated for tongue and teeth optical biomarkers — coating, hue shift, gingival signal, plaque indicators. Trend data is more reliable than single readings; we surface weekly summaries rather than alarming day-to-day spikes. Not a diagnostic device; share results with your dentist for clinical interpretation.

    When do refills ship?

    Your initial bundle includes a 90-day supply of Foam + Mouthpiece. After that, Quarterly Refills (on Subscribe & Save or added as a $65/quarter subscription on One Time) ship automatically every 90 days. First refill arrives 90 days after your original order.
